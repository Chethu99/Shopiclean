Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

1. Client-Side Architecture & Zero Data Collection

At ShopiClean.com, privacy is guaranteed by system architecture. All spreadsheet evaluations, column sanitization, image validations, and variant partitions are computed strictly in your device's browser memory (RAM) using standard client-side JavaScript APIs.

Your CSV files, catalog matrices, and customer data are never uploaded, transmitted, or hosted on our servers.

2. Payment Processing

Financial transactions are handled through certified checkout infrastructure. ShopiClean does not collect, record, or store raw debit/credit card numbers or sensitive billing credentials.

3. Analytics & Telemetry

We do not run invasive tracking cookies, behavioral tracking scripts, or sell data to third-party brokers. Standard anonymous server access logs (IP addresses and page response metrics) are collected strictly for network security and DDoS mitigation via CDN infrastructure.

4. Contact Information

For questions regarding our privacy architecture or policy, contact: support@shopiclean.com.
