Our Client-Side Zero-Storage Promise

ShopiClean does not operate file upload servers. Your spreadsheet data—including confidential wholesale prices, supplier identities, SKUs, inventory counts, and customer fields—is parsed and sanitized entirely in your local web browser’s volatile memory.

1. Information We Never Collect or Store

When you use any utility across the ShopiClean platform (including the CSV Validator, Supplier Converter, Variant Splitter, or Image Checker), your spreadsheet files are never transmitted to our web servers, database clusters, or third-party cloud storage bucket providers:

  • We do not log, read, or copy your catalog product details or supplier names.
  • We do not track or record your cost of goods, selling prices, or profit margins.
  • We do not store or mirror the image URLs listed inside your media columns.
  • All file reading and transformation takes place via standard Web APIs inside your browser tab. Closing or refreshing the page instantly clears this data from memory.

2. Local Storage Usage

ShopiClean uses your browser’s native localStorage API for the single operational purpose of managing your utility pass:

  • shopiclean_license: Stores your encrypted alphanumeric license key and associated expiration timestamp so you can access exports across all four utilities during your 7-day access window without having to re-enter your key on every page load.

You can inspect or delete this key at any time by clearing your browser cache or site data via your browser developer settings.

3. Payment Information & Order Processing

Payment processing and automated license key delivery are handled by our merchant of record partner, Dodo Payments.

ShopiClean never collects, processes, transmits, or stores credit card numbers, debit cards, or bank authentication details on our infrastructure. When purchasing a 7-day pass, your billing details and email address are provided directly to Dodo Payments under their own secure PCI-compliant privacy protocols.

4. Server Logs & Analytics

Like all standard web servers, our hosting infrastructure automatically captures basic non-identifiable technical headers when you request an HTML file, such as browser type, operating system, and approximate geographical region (country level). These logs do not contain personal identifiers and are never linked to any CSV files or content you inspect on the site.
