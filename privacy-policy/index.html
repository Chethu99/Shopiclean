Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

1. Core Architecture: Client-Side Processing

ShopiClean is engineered around a fundamental privacy principle: zero server data ingestion.

When you upload, drag, or drop a CSV or spreadsheet file into our tools (CSV Auditor & Fixer, Supplier Catalog Converter, Variant Splitter & Linker, or Image URL Scanner), the file is read and parsed exclusively inside your device's web browser memory using client-side JavaScript.

Important Guarantee: Your inventory catalog files, customer records, supplier names, pricing matrices, margins, and proprietary data are never uploaded, transmitted, viewed, or stored on any remote ShopiClean web server or database.

2. External Network Communications & Payment Processing

While spreadsheet processing is 100% local, the website interacts with external services in only two specific instances:

  • Payment Processing: All financial transactions, card billing, and checkout flows are handled directly by our Merchant of Record and payment processor, Dodo Payments. We do not collect, view, or store your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or bank details.
  • License Key Validation: When you purchase or restore access to a 7-Day Launch Pass, your browser communicates with Dodo Payments’ secure license API endpoints to verify license key validity, expiration dates, and activation quotas.

3. Local Browser Storage (localStorage)

ShopiClean uses your browser’s standard web storage mechanism (window.localStorage) exclusively to preserve your active pass credentials across browser reloads. We store only:

  • Your alphanumeric license key
  • The license activation timestamp and pass expiration date
  • Temporary local preferences (such as column re-mapping states)

This data remains confined to your workstation and is never combined with third-party tracking profiles. You can purge this data at any time by clearing your browser cookies and site storage.

4. Customer Support Inquiries

If you contact our support desk at support@shopiclean.com for technical assistance or refund processing, we receive your email address, your inquiry text, and any sample CSV files or error logs you voluntarily choose to attach.

Any sample spreadsheet files sent directly to our support desk are utilized solely for diagnosing import failures and are deleted within 30 days of resolution.

5. Web Analytics & Cookies

We do not use invasive third-party cross-site behavioral tracking cookies. Any analytical logging employed is strictly privacy-compliant, aggregate-level server telemetry (such as page visit counts and browser user-agent types) used to optimize page performance and server uptime.
