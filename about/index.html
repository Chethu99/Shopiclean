About ShopiClean

Built for speed, zero-cloud privacy, and error-free catalog migrations.

Why ShopiClean Exists

Every e-commerce merchant who has migrated catalog data, launched wholesale lines, or updated product feeds has encountered Shopify's notorious spreadsheet errors: "Illegal quoting on line 42", "Price is not a number", or unlinked handles that accidentally disintegrate multi-row variants.

Existing solutions typically require either hours of manual troubleshooting in Microsoft Excel or installing invasive Shopify store apps that demand full read/write API access to customer databases and product inventory.

ShopiClean offers a third alternative: dedicated, browser-native utility tools that perform instant audit checks and format repairs locally on your machine without requiring store permissions.

The 100% In-Browser Privacy Guarantee

Unlike legacy cloud converters that upload your wholesale pricing, supplier names, and product matrices to central databases, ShopiClean executes 100% of its parsing routines directly inside your web browser’s memory using client-side JavaScript.

When you drag and drop a spreadsheet into ShopiClean, your file never travels across the internet to our servers. Your proprietary commercial data never leaves your workstation.

Independent & Lightweight

ShopiClean is built by independent web engineers focused on practical, high-utility developer tools. We are not backed by venture capital, we do not lock customers into unwanted monthly recurring subscriptions, and we do not sell data. A single $9 pass provides complete, unrestricted access for 7 days to clean and migrate your catalog files.
