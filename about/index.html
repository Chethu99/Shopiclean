About ShopiClean

High-performance catalog utilities built for operational operators.

The Problem with Traditional CSV Editing

E-commerce managers and catalog teams lose hours battling delicate spreadsheet errors: Excel auto-converts barcode SKUs into scientific notation, corrupted UTF-8 quotes freeze Shopify’s background import jobs, and third-party supplier files fail due to misaligned headers.

Most existing solutions require connecting bloated multi-tenant store apps, granting third-party servers wide read/write access to confidential product matrices, pricing strategies, and customer orders.

The Zero-Server Guarantee

ShopiClean takes a strictly architectural approach to operational security: zero server-side data ingestion. Every calculation, column alignment, text sanitization step, and file export executes entirely inside your client browser’s local memory (RAM).

No Store Credentials

We never request API access tokens, store passwords, or merchant authorizations.

Absolute Privacy

Your catalogs and supplier margins remain strictly on your local computer.
