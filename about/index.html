About ShopiClean

Practical utilities for e-commerce operators

Our Focus

Importing large inventory spreadsheets into e-commerce stores should not require hours of manual debugging in Excel or complex custom scripts. A single missing handle or rogue currency sign shouldn't halt a product launch.

ShopiClean.com builds hyper-focused, browser-native web tools designed to solve specific catalog roadblocks instantly.

Engineered for Performance & Privacy

Unlike conventional web applications that upload your supplier sheets to unknown cloud servers, ShopiClean runs completely inside your local browser memory. That means:

  • Zero Latency: 10,000+ rows parsed and repaired in milliseconds.
  • Absolute Confidentiality: Your supplier pricing and catalogs never leave your computer.
  • Reliability: Works with zero backend server dependencies.
