Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I test my CSV file for free before buying a pass?

Yes. ShopiClean offers a completely free error scan and audit for your entire CSV file. You can preview the detected issues and export up to 5 repaired rows for free to verify that Shopify accepts the format before upgrading to the 7-day pass.

Do I need to install any apps or give access to my Shopify store?

No. ShopiClean requires zero Shopify store access, API keys, or permissions. It is an independent browser utility: you inspect and repair your catalog file directly on this page, and then upload the verified CSV into your store yourself.

Is my customer or supplier data private?

Yes, 100%. All CSV file parsing, data transformations, and exports occur completely inside your browser memory via client-side JavaScript. Your spreadsheet data is never sent to or stored on any server. The only external network requests made by this site are to our payment provider (Dodo Payments) to process checkouts and verify license key status.

What common Shopify CSV errors does this fix?

It resolves illegal currency symbols ($/€/£) in price columns, missing or unlinked Handle slugs across multi-row variants, quote mismatches that shift columns, missing inventory tracker policies, local workstation file paths (like C:\\), and invalid image URL formatting.

What if the repaired file still won't import?

We stand behind our tools. If your repaired CSV fails to upload properly to Shopify after following our guidelines, send your original file, repaired export, and Shopify Admin error log to support@shopiclean.com within 14 days for a full refund. (Excludes account-level store limits, third-party app conflicts, and files exceeding 15MB).
