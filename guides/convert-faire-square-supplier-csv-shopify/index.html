Why Supplier CSVs Fail to Import Directly

Wholesale marketplaces and POS systems export inventory using their own internal database naming conventions. When you try to upload a raw export directly into Shopify Admin, the upload fails with messages like "Missing required header: Handle" or "Illegal column count".

Shopify's CSV importer expects exact, case-sensitive column headers. If a supplier labels their price column as MSRP or Cost instead of Variant Price, Shopify ignores the column entirely, resulting in products with $0.00 pricing.

Key Column Mapping Reference

Third-Party Supplier Header Official Shopify CSV Header Required Formatting
Product Name / Item Title Plain text, double-quotes escaped
Item Name / Slug (None) Handle kebab-case URL slug (auto-generated)
Retail Price / MSRP Variant Price Numeric decimal only (no $, €, or £)
Current Stock / Quantity Variant Inventory Qty Integer numbers only
Barcode / UPC Variant Barcode 12 or 13-digit text (preserve leading zeros)
