Frequently Asked Questions

Does Shopify accept relative paths like `/images/product.jpg`? ▼

No. Shopify requires absolute publicly accessible URLs starting with http:// or https:// so its crawler can fetch and host the file. Relative or local filesystem paths are discarded.

Does this tool re-host my photos? ▼

No. The tool cleans, encodes, and formats your existing image URLs inside the CSV. When you import the CSV into Shopify, Shopify's servers fetch and host the images.
